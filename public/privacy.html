Privacy notice

Revive Co collects the information needed to answer inquiries, schedule and provide cleaning services, process payments, and operate this website. This notice explains the categories of information involved and the choices available to you.

Information you provide

When you inquire or book, the site may collect your name, email, phone number, service address, property and appointment details, service notes, communication preferences, and transaction status. Do not submit alarm codes, lock codes, financial account details, or other sensitive information in free-text fields.

Payments

Eligible online payments are processed by Stripe. Revive Co does not receive or store your full card number or wallet credentials. Stripe may collect payment, billing, fraud-prevention, and device information under its own privacy terms.

Website measurement

The Sales Vision implementation records first-party, pseudonymous events such as page views, booking steps, referral source, campaign parameters, and completed actions. It uses an anonymous session identifier and does not place contact-form text, addresses, or payment credentials into analytics events.

How information is used

  • Respond to requests and communicate about service.
  • Manage availability, bookings, customers, payments, and business records.
  • Understand which pages and marketing channels help customers.
  • Protect the website, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations.

Service providers and retention

Information may be processed by hosting, database, analytics, communications, and payment providers working for Revive Co. Records are retained for as long as reasonably needed for service, accounting, dispute, security, and legal purposes.

Your choices

Marketing consent is optional. You may ask to correct or delete eligible personal information or opt out of marketing by calling (480) 582-5615. Some transaction or tax records may need to be retained.

Questions
