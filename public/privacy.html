Privacy Notice

Revive Co uses the information needed to provide cleaning services, manage customer relationships, answer questions, operate this website, and protect the business. This notice explains the main categories of information involved and the choices available to you.

BookingKoala Forms and Accounts

The booking, login, signup, contact, and live-review experiences on this website are embedded from Revive Co's BookingKoala account. Information entered inside those frames is transmitted directly to BookingKoala and made available to Revive Co for service operations. Depending on the feature you use, this may include your name, email, phone number, service address, appointment preferences, selected services and extras, account authentication data, messages, booking history, and feedback.

BookingKoala may also process device, usage, approximate location, cookie, and referral information under its own privacy policy.

Payments

If a booking requires payment details, those details are handled by the payment processor connected to BookingKoala. Revive Co's website does not receive or store your full card number. BookingKoala and Revive Co may receive limited payment information such as the payment status, transaction reference, card brand, or last four digits when needed to administer a booking.

Reviews

If you submit feedback after service, the review and the configured version of your name may be displayed in Revive Co's live review feed. BookingKoala allows Revive Co to configure a rating threshold and whether the feed shows a full name, first name with last initial, or no name.

Website Measurement

The Sales Vision implementation records first-party, pseudonymous events such as page views, referral source, campaign parameters, and completed link actions. It uses an anonymous session identifier and does not place contact-form text, booking details, account credentials, or payment information into those events. Activity inside a cross-origin BookingKoala frame may be measured separately by BookingKoala and any analytics tools configured there.

How Information Is Used

  • Schedule, deliver, change, and support cleaning services.
  • Prepare pricing, process authorized payments, and maintain customer accounts.
  • Send service, account, and appointment communications.
  • Answer questions and resolve disputes.
  • Measure website and marketing performance.
  • Prevent fraud, protect the website, and comply with legal obligations.

Service Providers and Retention

Information may be processed by providers that support booking, hosting, databases, analytics, communications, and payments. Records are retained for as long as reasonably needed for service, accounting, dispute, security, and legal purposes. Each provider may also have retention obligations described in its own policies.

Your Choices

You can update many account and booking details through the customer login. You may also ask Revive Co to correct or delete eligible personal information by calling (480) 582-5615. Some business records may need to be retained. Browser settings can limit cookies, but doing so may affect embedded login, booking, payment, or account features; each embedded page also includes a direct-open fallback.
