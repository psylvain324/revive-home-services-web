Privacy Notice

Revive Co collects the information needed to answer service requests, provide cleaning services, and operate this website. This notice explains the categories of information involved and the choices available to you.

Information You Provide

When you send a service request, the site may collect your name, email address, phone number, requested service, property details, preferred frequency, add-on interests, and the message you provide. Do not submit alarm codes, lock codes, financial account details, or other sensitive information in free-text fields.

Email Delivery

The website sends the details of your request to Revive Co through an email delivery provider so the team can respond. Your email address is used as the reply address for that notification.

Website Measurement

The Sales Vision implementation records first-party, pseudonymous events such as page views, request steps, referral source, campaign parameters, and completed actions. It uses an anonymous session identifier and does not place contact-form text or contact details into analytics events.

How Information Is Used

  • Respond to requests and communicate about service.
  • Prepare quotes and discuss service scope and availability.
  • Understand which pages and marketing channels help customers.
  • Protect the website, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations.

Service Providers and Retention

Information may be processed by hosting, database, analytics, and email providers working for Revive Co. Records are retained for as long as reasonably needed for service, accounting, dispute, security, and legal purposes.
