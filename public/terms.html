Website and booking terms

These terms apply to requests submitted through the Revive Co website. They are intended to make the online process clear; Revive Co will confirm the final service scope, price, access plan, and any property-specific terms directly with you.

Appointment requests

Selecting an available time and receiving a request confirmation reserves the time shown, subject to Revive Co confirming that the selected service and duration fit the property. Quote-based, commercial, move, and post-construction work may require additional review before the appointment is final.

Pricing and payment

Online prices are starting estimates based on the selected service and add-ons. Conditions that materially differ from the booking details may require approval of a revised scope or price. When offered, online payments are processed securely by Stripe. Any applicable tax is shown at checkout when tax calculation is enabled.

Deposits

If a deposit is shown at checkout, it is applied toward the booked service. The remaining balance and its due date will be confirmed by Revive Co. Keep the Stripe receipt and Revive confirmation code for your records.

Access and safety

You are responsible for providing safe, lawful access to the service property and accurately disclosing conditions that could affect the work, including pets, hazards, restricted areas, utilities, or active construction. Do not enter alarm or lock codes in public website forms; arrange sensitive access details directly with Revive Co.

Changes and cancellations

Call (480) 582-5615 promptly to request a reschedule or cancellation. Revive Co will explain any applicable cancellation, late-change, access, or deposit terms before enforcing them. Website submission alone does not create a cancellation policy that was not disclosed to you.

Tax planning disclaimer
